SEO & GEO · Content workflow

Answer buyer questions. Help them find ABC.

Six simple steps: find the right questions, answer them with real ABC examples, share the answers, and keep improving them.

Explore the workflow
  1. 01
    Discover

    Find the questions buyers are asking

    Check Semrush, Google Search Console, website results, and customer questions. Look for questions from builders, GCs, developers, and designers that our website has not answered well.

  2. 02
    Audit

    Check whether we already answered it

    Could an existing blog or service page answer the question better? Update that page first. Write a new blog only when it answers a different question.

  3. 03
    Use real evidence

    Use real examples from ABC

    Choose a real project, factory process, material, customer comment, or event ABC took part in. Plan a mix of design tips, factory work, buyer guides, and company news. A public event listing alone does not mean ABC attended.

  4. 04
    Create

    Create one clear answer on the website

    Answer the main question first, then explain the choices. Make one page for one question and prepare:

    Page title · web address · category · introductionSeparate Google title and descriptionFull article · clear section headingsRelated links · FAQsQuestions people search or ask AITwo landscape images (4:3) + image descriptions
  5. 05
    Share & review

    Adapt and share it for each channel

    Give the full answer on the website. Use Instagram for photos and details, LinkedIn for project choices and how things are made, and KABi and GC channels for their own audiences. Check facts, images, repeated topics, and calls to action. Do not call a tool “AI” unless it really uses AI.

  6. 06
    Measure & improve

    Check what happened and improve the page

    Check separately: Is the page live? Does Google list it? Is it being seen or clicked? Does AI mention ABC? Does it bring useful inquiries? Improve pages that are seen but rarely clicked, especially ones close to the top of search results. Reuse good examples for different questions instead of making duplicate pages.

From HR pain point to shipped workflow

One employee, one guided intake, fewer repeated steps. Here’s how the project moved from a frontline problem to a live tool.

  1. 01
    Problem

    Repeated paperwork slowed every hire

    Employees re-entered personal details across forms. HR often had to explain form fields and safety information.

    About 30 minutes per employee
  2. 02
    Define

    Turn the pain into clear requirements

    Mapped existing forms, required fields, and rules. Specified one pre-hire Google Form, field matching, selected file generation, and a human review step.

  3. 03
    Configure

    Build the AI-assisted document flow

    Using Codex, configured the agent to read employee ID and SSN details, match submitted information to forms, and mark employee signature fields.

  4. 04
    Human check

    HR verifies before files are created

    HR reviews extracted details, chooses the needed forms, then generates and downloads them. Company and HR signature fields stay blank.

  5. 05
    Improve & launch

    Test edge cases; CS deploys the system

    Identified unreliable checkbox recognition, including marital status; shared examples and iterated with CS. The CS team deployed the system.

    My role: requirements, business rules, Codex configuration, debugging, and user feedback · CS: deployment
  6. 06
    Result · Per employee
    ~30min before
    →
    5–8min after
    22–25 min saved

    About 73%–83% less processing time, with less repeated entry and less step-by-step form guidance from HR. Human review remains in the workflow.
